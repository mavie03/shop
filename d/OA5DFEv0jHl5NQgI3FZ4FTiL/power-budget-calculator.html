12V Power Budget Calculator

For caravans, camper trailers and 4WDs in Australia. Works offline. Nothing you type leaves your device.

General information only. This is a planning estimate, not electrical design or installation advice. Have your final design and install checked by a licensed auto-electrician. Anything 240V/mains needs a licensed electrician. Prices and sun figures are estimates.

Always round up to the next standard size you can buy. If results look odd, check the hours and watts you entered. The DC-DC figure is a starting point only: check your alternator rating, whether it is a smart alternator, and the charger's specification.

4. What this does not cover

  • Cable sizes, fuse or breaker ratings and wiring layout. These are a safety matter for a licensed auto-electrician.
  • 240V appliances, inverters feeding mains circuits, gas or caravan compliance.
  • Shade, dust, panel angle and cloud. They can cut real solar output well below the sums above.

About the sun figures: the figures in the region list are long-term NASA POWER averages (all-sky solar irradiance on a flat surface, satellite-derived) for the darkest month of each season, rounded down a little. The Central Australia figure also agrees with Bureau of Meteorology Alice Springs Airport data. Real days vary with cloud, dust, shade and panel tilt, so leave margin. To check another place, look it up at power.larc.nasa.gov (long-term monthly values in kWh/m2/day, which equals peak sun hours), or take a BoM solar exposure figure in MJ/m2 per day and divide by 3.6.
